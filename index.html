Bookcase Math

The box says 100 books. Your books are not the box's books.

Boundary: counting arithmetic only Bookcase Math counts books, shelves, and inches of wall. It never tells you what a shelf can hold in weight or whether a bookcase is anchored - those belong to the manufacturer and a stud finder.

The shelf truth

Count the books in one foot of your own shelf - 9 is normal, the box photo used paperbacks turned sideways. Your 32-inch shelf at 9 books a foot holds 24.

The collection truth

60 books at 24 a shelf is 3 shelves, with 12 to spare. 49 books is already the second bookcase.

The wall truth

3 shelves of 32 inches need 8 feet of wall. Your 6.5 feet leaves 1.5 short - the hallway was always going to get one.

Run your numbers
Bookcase Math is exact arithmetic over your own counts and tape-measure numbers, nothing more.
